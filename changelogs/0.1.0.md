# Added

- Box select keybind for the FTB Quests editor, held while left-dragging and defaulting to Shift, remappable from the vanilla Controls screen. Panning is suppressed while the keybind is held, and the existing middle-mouse drag keeps working.
- Move selection keybind, held while left-clicking a quest or chapter image to pick up the current selection, defaulting to Shift and remappable from the vanilla Controls screen. Clicking something that is not selected yet adds it first, matching middle-click, and the existing middle-mouse pickup keeps working.
- Complete instantly keybind, held while left-clicking a quest to complete it on the spot instead of opening it, defaulting to C and remappable from the vanilla Controls screen. Editor mode only, and it completes the linked quest when the click lands on a quest link.
- Reset progress keybind, held while left-clicking a quest to clear its progress instead of opening it, defaulting to R and remappable from the vanilla Controls screen. It is the counterpart to the complete instantly keybind, and also clears a repeatable quest's cooldown.
- Delete keybind, held while left-clicking a quest or a chapter image to delete it instead of opening it, defaulting to F and remappable from the vanilla Controls screen. It deletes only the clicked object rather than the whole selection, deletes the link rather than its target when the click lands on a quest link, and asks for no confirmation.
- Numpad Enter accepted anywhere the quest book accepts Enter, so Shift + Numpad Enter saves a description edit or a colour picker exactly like Shift + Enter.
- Smart Filter keybind, pressed while looking at a quest to hand a creative-mode player an FTB Filter System smart filter matching any of the items its item tasks ask for. Defaults to Numpad +, remappable from the vanilla Controls screen, and tasks that already filter on a tag expand to the items they accept.
- Smart Filter keybind extended to the inventory and creative screens, where it hands a creative-mode player a filter matching the items in the hotbar, or every item in the inventory when Shift is held. Duplicates collapse into one entry and existing smart filters are left out of the result.
- Quarter-grid snapping in the FTB Quests editor, overriding the file's grid scale so dragged and pasted objects always land on a .0, .25, .5 or .75 coordinate regardless of their size. Holding Shift for free positioning still works.
- Save keybind for the FTB Quests editor, defaulting to Ctrl + S to save the quest file on the server and Ctrl + Shift + S to download a copy of the quest files to the client. Remappable from the vanilla Controls screen, and it does nothing outside editor mode.
- Split view keybind for the quest book, defaulting to V for two chapters side by side and Alt + V for one above the other, both remappable from the vanilla Controls screen. Each side keeps its own chapter, zoom, scroll position and selection, the divider between them can be dragged to resize, and clicking a side gives it the keyboard.
